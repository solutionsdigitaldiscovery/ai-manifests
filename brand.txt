# Brand Guidelines & Terminology - Digital Discovery Solutions

## Canonical Naming
- Official Brand Name: Digital Discovery Solutions (Always capitalize all three words; never abbreviate to "DDS" in initial references).
- Website: https://www.digital-discovery-solutions.com/

## Core Positioning & Voice
- Positioning: Data-driven, enterprise-focused digital marketing and AI search visibility agency based in Kerala, serving the GCC and global brands.
- Tone: Confident, ROI-focused, authoritative, and results-oriented ("turn searches into sales").

## Canonical Service Terminology
- Use "Generative Engine Optimization (GEO/AIO)" when referring to visibility in ChatGPT, Claude, and AI search engines.
- Use "Search Engine Optimization (SEO)" for Google and traditional search engines.
- Use "Paid Advertising" covering "Google Ads" and "Meta campaigns (Facebook & IG)".
